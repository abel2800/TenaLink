# Target Users

## Primary users
### Patients seeking medical care
These users need a straightforward path to find doctors, specialists, or clinics based on urgency, condition, or preferred location. They value quick access, trust signals, and easy booking guidance.

### Busy professionals
This group wants secure and efficient appointment planning that minimizes friction and allows them to act quickly without confusion. They often need information presented clearly and concisely.

## Secondary users
### Caregivers and family coordinators
These users often help elderly relatives, children, or dependents navigate healthcare choices. They need helpful filtering, service explanations, and reassurance.

### Clinic administrators
Although not the main interface audience in Week 1, clinic administrators would benefit from a future booking and provider management workflow. They need a modern patient experience that supports operational efficiency.

## Stakeholder considerations
- Patients expect convenience and trust.
- Providers need clear, professional presentation of services.
- The product must balance accessibility with premium healthcare aesthetics.
- Future lifecycle planning may include AI-assisted triage or scheduling, but that is intentionally deferred in Week 1.
